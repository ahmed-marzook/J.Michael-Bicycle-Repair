7. NOTES — decisions already made, for reference
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PRICES AND HOURS ARE EDITABLE WITHOUT A DEVELOPER.
  Both are driven by the published Google Sheet. Edit the sheet, and the
  live site picks it up. Two things to know:
    - The page must be reloaded to see it.
    - The "status_override" column forces the status badge regardless of
      the timetable. LEAVE IT EMPTY for normal automatic behaviour. It
      currently says OPEN, which means the badge will read "Open now"
      even at 3am on a Sunday until it is cleared.

* The site works fully without JavaScript, loads no third-party
  trackers, and sets no cookies of its own. The only outside content is
  the Google map on the home and contact pages.

* Opening hours are shown in UK time regardless of where the visitor is.

* We have NOT invented anything: no fake reviews, no made-up
  qualifications, no guessed opening hours, no "most popular" badge on a
  price tier we have no evidence for.
